# Nine new June scenes · 0.11.0

Choose **9–17 June** in the calendar. Eight new compact routes join the existing game, and 15 June's decoration and soundtrack are refreshed as **Windmill Heights**. There are now 29 playable dates, including every day from 1–17 June. The original 15 June collision layout, checkpoints, springs, lifts and hazards are preserved.

The supplied musical guides are creative references. The nine finished MP3s are copied unchanged and play in their matching dates. No supplied composition/rendering scripts were run, and the source folder was not modified. The future titles listed in `June_Level_Plan.md` do not create or rename other stages in this build.

| Date | Scene | Distinctive decoration and progression |
|---|---|---|
| 9 · The Glasshouse Run | A high glasshouse with reflected summer light | Glazed roof bays, palms and fern beds frame ceramic steps; the route opens from the glass door into layered galleries. |
| 10 · Cloverfield Crossing | Intersecting paths in an open clover common | Crossing paths, small timber bridges, pale fingerposts and low hedges lead towards the field beyond the hedge. |
| 11 · Riverside Rush | A broad, fast silver river | A broad river landscape, animated rapids, moored boats and riverside timber distinguish this from June 2's small willow stream. |

| 14 · The Woodland Detour | Cool leaf shade with a sunny clearing | Tall trunks, foxgloves, fallen wood and small mushrooms frame a middle clearing before the canopy returns. |
| 15 · Windmill Heights | High sails against a broad summer horizon | Larger animated mill towers, turning distant sails and wind ribbons decorate the existing lift-and-bellflower layout. |
| 16 · The Hayfield Hop | Freshly cut hay rows in warm afternoon light | Round decorative hay rolls, long stubble rows and weathered barns surround square playable hay blocks. |
| 17 · Brookside Bounce | Small cascades and a sheltered willow pool | Stepped brooks, rounded stone arches, lilies and dragonflies surround pebble steps and optional existing bellflower springs. |

Each date has a dedicated generated pixel-art landscape plate, with original native drawing code adding animated landmarks, water, foliage and small atmospheric details. Each combines its own background composition, landmark vocabulary, spatial arrangement, palette and source music. The river, orchard and willow callbacks intentionally develop earlier June places. No reference-game assets were copied. The exact image-generation prompts, saved paths and hashes are recorded in `art/june/PROVENANCE_09_17.json`; the nine plates are `art/june/06-09.png` through `06-17.png`.

## Readability and controls

All authored decoration is behind collision terrain, the player and hazards. Decorative ladders, glass frames, arch bridges and round hay rolls have no collision. The usable route keeps the familiar 48-pixel square blocks, dark frames and bright top caps. Low planting leaves space around spikes. Reduced motion freezes scenic animation.

Running, jumping, charge-dash tuning, the extended Dash Lab and underwater November are unchanged. The new dates enable the existing charge dash. The scenic June water remains decorative; it does not activate November's swimming controller.

The eight new routes are 6,720 logical pixels long, with two checkpoints each. They are decoration and layout references, not finished five-minute daily stages. 15 June retains its existing 10,800-pixel route. The full supplied tracks have composed endings; the music player restarts them if the player remains in a stage, without claiming a seamless loop. Section placement is spatial, not beat-synchronized.

## Editing and adding days

- `content/stages/06-09.json` through `06-17.json` store complete per-day scene and route data.
- `ambience.june_scene` selects one of nine entries in `content/june_scenes.json`.
- `scripts/june_scenery.gd` draws the day-specific distant compositions and new prop families. Shared props remain in the existing scenery modules.
- `terrain_style` selects the day's palette; geometry and controller settings are independent.
- `content/june_09_17_sources.json` records each musical guide, source track, copied track hash, scene and distant plate. Each stage also records its track provenance in `identity`.

The workshop lists all 29 dates and tracks. Copy a sample, move or paint blocks and Playtest to view its decoration. Export/reopen preserves scene keys, custom landmarks, music and atmosphere. New layouts choose the first unfinished calendar date, currently 18 June. Existing saved drafts keep their own data. Specialized props remain editable through JSON; the small workshop's general tree/flower brushes are unchanged.
